Stage history

← Dashboard

The dashboard records when each item's stage changes in Printer's Plan or Atlas (Atlas orders show as A268506 and so on). It checks every open job about once a minute, so each time is when the change was seen and is accurate to about a minute. The events download also has a "Changed after" column, the last time the old stage was seen. The real change happened between the two times. Times are Pacific. Wait times count shop hours only (Mon–Fri, 8am–4pm), so a job that waits from Friday 3pm to Monday 9am shows 2 hours; hover a wait time to see the plain clock time. Waits over one work day (8 hours) are highlighted.

Add dates from Printer's Plan

For stage changes from before tracking started, paste dates from Printer's Plan (or straight from Excel). They're stored as dates only and show in italics. A time the dashboard recorded always wins over a typed date. Either layout works:

One line per date: 267130, Pre Press, 10/14  ·  267130 Press 10/15/26  ·  add an item number if needed: 267130, 2, Press, 10/15

Or one row per job with a header row: Job | Item | Approved | Pre Press | Press | Left Press (Item optional; blank cells are skipped)

Stages: Approved (left Not Approved), Pre Press, Press (or Indigo / Ricoh / Konica), Left Press. With no item number, the date applies to every item on the job. Pasting a date again for the same job and stage replaces it.
