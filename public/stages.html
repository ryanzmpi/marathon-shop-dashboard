The dashboard records when each item's stage changes in Printer's Plan. It checks every open job about once a minute, so each time is when the change was seen and is accurate to about a minute. The events download also has a "Changed after" column, the last time the old stage was seen. The real change happened between the two times. Times are Pacific.
